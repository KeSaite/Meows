Meows

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Meows is a native Android app that connects directly to servers over SSH. Server settings, credentials, and monitoring history are stored on your device. If you turn on Google Drive backup, the backup is encrypted on the device before it is uploaded to the app-private storage area.

Information Stored

Everything below is kept locally on your device:

  • Server connection details: SSH host, port, username, and your password or private key (encrypted).
  • Monitoring data: performance metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network, TCP connections) read from the servers you add.
  • Your settings: alert thresholds, notification preferences, and app options you configure.

How the Data Is Used

  • To open SSH connections to the servers you add.
  • To show live status and history charts.
  • To raise notifications when a threshold is crossed or a server goes offline. This is handled entirely on your device. There is no push server.
  • To create or restore an optional encrypted backup in Google Drive app-private storage, only after you sign in and choose to use it.
  • To show the geographic location of server IP addresses, using an offline database.

Storage & Security

  • Passwords and private keys are encrypted with AES-GCM using keys held in the Android Keystore. An optional master password adds a second layer.
  • Server settings and monitoring data are stored locally. Optional backups are encrypted on the device before upload.
  • Monitoring data is kept only for a short rolling window on your device, then removed automatically.
  • No monitoring data is sent to any external server.
  • SSH connections go directly from your device to your servers.

Third-Party Services

Meows has no backend of its own. One optional online integration and one bundled database involve third-party services or data:

  • Google Drive API: optional backup, restore, and deletion. It is used only after you sign in and grant consent. Backups are encrypted on the device and stored in Google Drive app-private storage; they are managed from Meows. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Offline IP database: an IPInfo Lite database bundled inside the app provides IP geolocation. Lookups happen on your device; nothing is sent to any geolocation service.

What I Do Not Do

  • I do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • I do not share your credentials or monitoring data with anyone.
  • I do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or any device identifier.
  • I do not show advertisements.

Permissions

Meows requests a single runtime permission (notifications), and only so it can alert you. You can deny it and keep using the app; you simply will not receive notification alerts.

You can also turn on an optional privacy mode that hides Meows from the recent-apps screen and blocks screenshots and screen recording.

Your Choices

  • Review, restore, or delete an optional Google Drive backup from Meows.
  • Delete local app data by removing servers or uninstalling Meows.
  • Turn features such as notifications or backup on or off at any time.
  • Choose which servers are monitored.

Data Retention

  • Server configurations are kept until you delete them.
  • Monitoring history is kept only briefly, then cleaned automatically.
  • Uninstalling Meows removes its local app data. Optional Google Drive backups are separate and are not deleted automatically.

Children's Privacy

Meows is not intended for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
